The issues most likely to change the decision

Dexito enters the shortlist, but ₹13.68 lakh is not the tax-inclusive planning figure

Its listed work adds to ₹13,67,691. Adding the stated 18% GST gives ₹16,13,875.38; ask for an explicit tax-inclusive total. It includes several reference details and countertops that others leave vague, but no measurements, defined utility/bathroom quantities or completion period. It also requires 100% payment before final fittings.

Happy Living’s total already includes the priced optional sections

₹8,20,078 base + ₹2,38,220 ceiling/electrical/paint/protection + ₹1,84,310 finishing allowances + ₹47,000 civil/plumbing = ₹12,89,608. Do not add factory shutter edging, profile doors, design or lighting twice. General soft-close, client stone/tile/sink purchases, deep cleaning, taxes and omitted reference units remain outside this number.

Happy Living’s lower price comes with a smaller scope

It is now rank 6, ahead of Casa but behind the five shortlisted offers, including Dexito. Its utility is only one 8.8 sq ft box, the third bathroom vanity is absent, and the drawing mirror wall, serving counter, proper TV storage and full U-shaped kitchen are not demonstrated. Its BWR dry-area plywood and general non-soft-close fittings also differ from the leading offers. Good directory ratings support a site visit; they do not close these gaps.

Like-for-like price is not yet available

The quotes differ in scope, quantities, finish and tax treatment. The report only adds supplier-listed allowances and corrects arithmetic; it does not invent a completion budget for unspecified work. A plus sign means unresolved money remains.

Gautam and Vighnaharta may be related

Both documents list 9651202712. Keep both submitted offers visible, but do not count them as independent competitive bids until the business relationship and scope are confirmed.

Measurements are provisional and sometimes inconsistent

The brief lists 1,926 sq ft saleable and 1,335 sq ft carpet area. Its separate measured-area sheet totals 1,346.47 sq ft and contains decimal/feet-inch ambiguity. None is an automatic false-ceiling billing quantity. Ceilings range from 712 sq ft at Lucky to 2,166.8 sq ft at Casa; returns, drops and coves need separate measured treatment.

The visual finish is more detailed than ordinary laminate boxes

White-and-gold mouldings, framed/mirrored doors, glass profiles, lit shelves and the pooja relief need exact finish samples and a priced drawing. A 1 mm laminate inclusion alone does not promise these details.

Plywood product names are not certificates

IS 303 can include BWP general-purpose plywood; marine plywood has the separate IS 710 standard. Century currently describes Sainik 710 as complying with IS 303 BWP, despite 710 in its product name. Require the sheet label, current product datasheet, grade, manufacturer and invoice rather than rejecting or accepting a board solely on the number.

Payment exposure differs considerably

D’LIFE asks for all but ₹20,000 before dispatch, IRIS all money before finishing, Dexito all money before final fittings (90% by halfway), Rohit nearly all money before shutter skins and Mega 70% on order. Negotiate a visible installation milestone and a meaningful snag-completion balance. A long plywood warranty is not the same as a workmanship warranty.

What to settle before choosing

  1. Use one marked-up floor plan and one unit schedule for all vendors. Record width, height and depth in millimetres, with separate box, frame/shutter, panel and running-foot quantities. Include a room-by-room ceiling take-off.
  2. Confirm all reference elements individually: drawing mirror wall, serving counter, usable TV storage, exact corner pooja, kitchen, three wardrobes/studies/dressing, four vanity areas and complete utility including stainless steel and drying rack.
  3. Freeze plywood maker/product/grade/thickness, shutter substrate, back-panel thickness, laminate brands and sheet-price limits, edge-band thickness, hinge/channel/tandem models, load ratings and soft-close exceptions.
  4. Specify the complete wet-area system: base material, edges/sealing, supports, stone or steel top, sink/tap, plumbing access and leak-related service responsibility. A material label alone does not establish the complete assembly.
  5. Ask for one final payable total with tax basis, design, stone, tiles, plumbing, electrical materials/labour, every light/driver, paint, protection, carrying charges, debris disposal and deep cleaning shown. Mark optional extras separately.
  6. For each shortlisted vendor, inspect one live site and at least two occupied homes, including one a year or more old. Speak with the owners about final versus initial price, finishing defects, delays and service response. Verify the actual site supervisor.
  7. Specify finish samples, approval/change-control process, completion date and dependencies, workmanship coverage, brand warranty documents and staged payments with money remaining for snag closure.
